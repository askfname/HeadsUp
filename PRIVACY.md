# HeadsUp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-30

Developer: PlayLab

HeadsUp detects when you are using your phone while walking and reminds you to look up. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

## Data the app accesses

- **Physical activity** — step-detector and activity-recognition signals are used only to detect walking.
- **Location** — while indoor/outdoor detection is enabled, the app accesses precise location, including in the background, to determine whether you are indoors and to mute reminders there. Location is processed only on your device; it is never uploaded, stored remotely, or shared by the app.
- **Screen, lock and proximity state** — used on-device to avoid disturbing you when the screen is off, the device is locked, or the phone is in a pocket.

## What the app does not collect

- No account, advertising ID, analytics or tracking.
- No location history, step data or device data is transmitted off your device by the app.
- No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Permissions

- **Physical activity** — walking detection.
- **Notifications** — delivering reminders.
- **Location (“while in use” or “all the time”)** — indoor/outdoor detection. “All the time” is required while monitoring runs in the background. You can turn off indoor muting or revoke the permission at any time.
- **Display over other apps** — pop-up and full-screen floating reminders.
- **Foreground service** — keeps walking detection running while monitoring is on.

## Data retention and deletion

All detection data is processed in memory on your device. Settings are stored locally until you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

## Third-party services

Google Play services (Activity Recognition) may be used on-device to improve walking detection. Its handling of data is governed by Google's privacy policy. The app does not send your data to the developer.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this URL.

# 看路提醒 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-30

开发者：PlayLab

看路提醒会在你走路使用手机时提醒你抬头看路。本政策说明应用访问哪些数据以及如何处理这些数据。

## 应用访问的数据

- **身体活动** — 步伐传感器与活动识别信号仅用于检测步行。
- **位置信息** — 开启室内外识别期间，应用会访问精确位置（包括后台位置），用于判断你是否在室内并在室内暂停提醒。位置信息仅在你的设备上处理，应用不会上传、远程存储或共享。
- **屏幕、锁屏与距离状态** — 仅在本机使用，用于在灭屏、锁屏或手机在口袋中时避免打扰你。

## 应用不会收集的数据

- 不收集账号、广告标识、统计分析或追踪信息。
- 应用不会将位置历史、步数或设备数据传输到你的设备之外。
- 不包含第三方广告或统计分析 SDK。

## 权限说明

- **身体活动** — 检测步行。
- **通知** — 发送提醒。
- **位置（“仅使用时”或“始终允许”）** — 室内外识别。监测在后台运行时需要“始终允许”。你可以随时关闭室内不提醒或撤销该权限。
- **悬浮窗（显示在其他应用上层）** — 弹窗与全屏悬浮提醒。
- **前台服务** — 在监测开启期间保持步行检测运行。

## 数据保留与删除

所有检测数据仅在设备内存中处理。设置保存在本机，直至你清除应用数据或卸载应用。

## 第三方服务

应用可能在本机使用 Google Play 服务（活动识别）来改进步行检测，其数据处理受 Google 隐私政策约束。应用不会将你的数据发送给开发者。

## 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会在知情情况下收集儿童的个人数据。

我们可能更新本政策，最新版本始终可通过本链接查看。
